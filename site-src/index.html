Fandomonium

Movie, TV and anime trivia for 2 to 4 teams around one screen, with your phones as controllers and a host with opinions. {{TOTAL}} questions across {{NFAN}} fandoms, from Harry Potter and Marvel to Pokémon, The Office and One Piece.

Your phone is the controller

Like a party game console, minus the console. Nobody installs anything: phones join in the browser, over Wi-Fi or mobile data.

  • Turn on Play with phones. The TV shows a QR code and a four-letter room code.
  • Scan to join. Type your name and pick your team. Several people can share a team.
  • Pick, answer, buzz. On your team’s turn, choose the fandom and answer on your phone. In All play, slam the buzzer and type scramble answers in.
  • The first to join is the VIP. They start rounds and move things along from the couch.

Six kinds of round

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed, which gets harder every round. Every round, each team gets a question of its own on the clock, and if they miss it, the other teams can buzz in to steal it for half the points. Then comes All play: a puzzle reveals itself bit by bit, its value drops from 500 to 100, and the first team to buzz gets a shot.

Made for the TV

Hook a laptop or PC up to the TV. Play with phones, or crowd around one keyboard: the buzz keys sit at the four corners, so nobody fights over the same spot. On a touchscreen, tap the big buzzers instead.

  • QR codePhones join from the lobby screen
  • F11Full screen in the Windows app (or use the Full screen button)
  • Q P Z MBuzz in for teams 1 to 4
  • 1–4Pick an answer, or tap it
  • SpaceNext question, or skip the curtain

Add your own questions

Every question lives in one file, src/bank.js. Add a line, push, and it’s in the game. Start with the difficulty from 1 to 3, put the right answer first, and the game shuffles the choices.

src/bank.js
hp:{
  mc:[
    [1, "What’s the name of Harry’s owl?",
     "Hedwig", "Errol", "Pigwidgeon", "Hermes"],
    // difficulty, question, right answer, three wrong ones
  ],
  scr:[[2, "HOGWARTS", "Place"]],
  who:[[3, "Dobby", ["clue 1", "clue 2", "clue 3", "clue 4"],
        ["Kreacher", "Winky", "Griphook"]],
}
Build it yourself (needs Node.js)
git clone https://github.com/matthewsleclair-hue/fandomonium
cd fandomonium
npm install
npm start      # play it in a window
npm run dist   # build release/Fandomonium.exe
